## About me

At Rocket Software, I help customers install, configure, and troubleshoot **Zowe, IBM Unified Management Server (UMS), and Db2 Admin Foundation**. I also develop internal tools that help support engineers make sense of mainframe logs and investigate problems.

As my team's **AI Champion** and a member of Rocket Software's **AI Innovators** group, I explore new AI tools, apply them to everyday workflows, and help colleagues use them effectively.

Teaching is a thread through my work. I previously mentored front-end developers at **Promineo Tech**, teaching React and modern web development and contributing to the AI Integration curriculum for its AI Engineering course. I also volunteer with **GiveCamp NWA**, building software for nonprofits in Northwest Arkansas.
